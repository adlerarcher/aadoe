Glossary

Systems GPCP reads, and the terms and acronyms spelled out.

Systems

What each named system is, and how this platform uses it. Agency reads arrive through SEPI, the DOE index. The schedule is computed from the encoded rule set.

This demonstration

SEPISubsurface Energy Permitting Index

A DOE index of federal and state agency systems. Connections are coordinated once. Coordinating platforms, leasing platforms, and other DOE platforms read from it, so partners work from authoritative agency streams. This platform is one of those. Records that arrive through SEPI are marked SEPI.

GPCPGeothermal Permitting Coordination Platform

The Geothermal Permitting Coordination Platform. This demonstration is GPCP: which reviews apply, who runs them, what waits on what, how long that takes.

MCPModel Context Protocol

The interface GPCP uses to read SEPI and to compute the encoded rule set. Each response carries its source name, endpoint, and retrieval timestamp. A static host cannot run those servers; this site publishes what they produced.

Federal systems and datasets

AFMSSAutomated Fluid Minerals Support System · BLM

BLM system of record for well records and Application for Permit to Drill status. Status arrives through SEPI.

BLM ePlanningBureau of Land Management NEPA docket

Public website for NEPA documents and project dockets. Status arrives through SEPI.

MLRSMineral and Land Records System · BLM

BLM system of record for lease serial, status, acreage, and administrative office. Records arrive through SEPI. Case names in those records are blank or null; some expiration dates fall in the year 3000.

Census TIGERwebTopologically Integrated Geographic Encoding and Referencing

Census Bureau geospatial service for federal American Indian reservation polygons. Records arrive through SEPI.

eCFRElectronic Code of Federal Regulations

Codified federal regulations. Title 43 implementing rules arrive through SEPI.

EPA UIC / SDWISUnderground Injection Control / Safe Drinking Water Information System

EPA systems for underground injection control permits. Status arrives through SEPI.

Federal RegisterOfficial journal of federal rules and notices

Public API for recent rules and proposed rules. Title 43 documents arrive through SEPI.

GeoPlatformFederal geospatial catalog

Catalog of federal geospatial services. Probed during connector inventory; the catalog API had moved as of 20 August 2026. Not a live source on this page.

GSA Site ScanningGeneral Services Administration

Federal website scanning program used in the connector inventory that classified public surfaces, missing APIs, and broken endpoints.

NDEPNevada Division of Environmental Protection

State agency for UIC and water-pollution permits. Status arrives through SEPI.

NDOMNevada Division of Minerals

State agency for the Permit to Drill, Geothermal Project Area Permit, Sundry Notice, and well completion report. Rule set v0.3.0 models those three Nevada rows alongside the federal geothermal drilling permit. Status arrives through SEPI.

NDWRNevada Division of Water Resources

State agency for water-right applications and decrees. Status arrives through SEPI. The GIS host did not resolve during inventory.

PermitAIPacific Northwest National Laboratory

PNNL program for federal environmental-review data and tools. NEPATEC is the document corpus. PermitTEC is the litigation corpus. This demonstration reads published extracts. It does not run PermitAI tools.

NEPATEC 2.0NEPA Text Corpus · PermitAI · Pacific Northwest National Laboratory

Extract of NEPA documents under PermitAI. The eight BLM geothermal Environmental Assessments on this site come from that corpus. No Nevada geothermal EA is in the extract. Marked Extracted.

NHDNational Hydrography Dataset · USGS

National map of surface waters. Named flowline segments arrive through SEPI.

NWINational Wetlands Inventory · USFWS

National map of wetlands, classified in the Cowardin system. Wetland records arrive through SEPI.

INFRA-COMPASSInfrastructure Continuous Ordinance Mapping · National Laboratories of the Rockies

Extraction of state and local codes and ordinances for energy infrastructure. Wind and solar siting ordinances are published. Geothermal zoning is in the FY26 program scope. Local ordinances would arrive through SEPI and COMPASS.

OpenEI RAPIDOpen Energy Information · Regulatory and Permitting Information Desktop Toolkit

DOE geothermal permitting reference covering 53 jurisdictions. Used here as the regulatory reference panel and as the check that found Nevada well permits missing from the rule set.

PAD-USProtected Areas Database of the United States · USGS

National inventory of protected lands. Wilderness Study Area records arrive through SEPI.

PermitTEC v0.1NEPA litigation corpus · Pacific Northwest National Laboratory

NEPA litigation corpus under PermitAI. 761 cases. No Nevada geothermal litigation appears in it. Two geothermal-and-Nevada matches were false positives.

SHPOState Historic Preservation Office · Nevada

State office that consults on historic properties under NHPA Section 106. Status arrives through SEPI.

USFWS IPaCInformation for Planning and Consultation

USFWS location API for species screening, field office, wetlands query, and designated critical habitat. Records arrive through SEPI.

USGS 3DEP3D Elevation Program

National elevation dataset. Used here as an optional hillshade on the map, off by default.
