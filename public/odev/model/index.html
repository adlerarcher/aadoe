Operating model

Strategy coordination, initiative delivery, and execution tracking each have a home.

Three homes

Coordinate · Deliver · Track

Each layer owns a different job so leadership sees the whole portfolio.

01 · Coordinate

Strategy Working Groups

Standing forums

Aligned to strategic themes and goals.

  • Coordinate across functions
  • Frame decisions
  • Surface dependencies
  • Maintain cross-office visibility
→
02 · Deliver

Strategic Initiatives

Lead-owned work

Bodies of work with defined scope and deliverables.

  • Identify primary and supporting working groups
  • Manage milestones
  • Resolve dependencies
  • Hand off outputs
→

Tracking Register
