About

The Index

A DOE index that connects to other agencies. Coordinate those connections once. Then build platforms on the index.

What this is

SEPI is a DOE index. It connects to the federal and state systems that hold subsurface energy authorizations — a lease serial, a well, a water right, a consultation, each in its own agency. Those connections are coordinated once.

Coordinating platforms, leasing platforms, and other DOE platforms can then be built on the index. Interagency partners get a single way in. The data stays with the agencies: authoritative streams, not a second file.
